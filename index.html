Cybersecurity Threat Directory

Updated definitions, mitigations, and reference articles from official security databases.

Malicious Software (Malware)

Encompasses viruses, trojans, worms, and spyware designed to infiltrate, damage, or compromise system integrity.

Ref: OWASP Top 10 CISA Malware Guide

Phishing & Social Engineering

Deceptive emails, calls, or messaging tricking victims into handing over credentials or confidential data.

Ref: NIST Special Pub CISA Phishing Defense

Ransomware Attacks

Extortion-focused malware encrypting files and demanding ransom payments for decryption keys.

Ref: StopRansomware.gov StopRansomware Hub

SQL Injection (SQLi)
